🏃 Sports Events

Students take part in athletics, football, volleyball, kho-kho, kabaddi, chess and yoga. The Annual Sports Meet and inter-house competitions help discover and nurture sporting talent, and students represent the school at block, district and state level.

🎭 Cultural Events

Independence Day, Republic Day, Rajyotsava, Annual Function and festivals are celebrated with songs, dance (including Chhattisgarhi folk forms like Karma and Suwa), drama and art competitions.

💡 Technical Events

Science exhibitions, quiz competitions, coding activities, robotics workshops and the INSPIRE-MANAK programme encourage innovation and scientific thinking.

⚜️ Scout Guide / NSS

Through Bharat Scouts & Guides and NSS, students learn discipline, first aid, teamwork and community service — including cleanliness drives, plantation and awareness rallies.
