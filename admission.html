Admission Process

1️⃣

Collect Form

Admission forms are available free of cost at the school office or as notified by the district.

2️⃣

Submit Form

Submit the filled form with required documents within the notified dates.

3️⃣

Selection

Where applications exceed seats, selection is done by lottery as per Government guidelines.

4️⃣

Confirmation

Selected students complete document verification and confirm admission.

Note: Admission is as per the guidelines issued by the School Education Department, Government of Chhattisgarh and the District Administration, Koriya. Education is free of cost. Please follow the official notice for exact dates.

Eligibility

  • Class 1: Child should be of the age prescribed by the State Government as on the cut-off date.
  • Classes 2–12: On the basis of vacant seats and previous class pass certificate.
  • Class 11: Stream allotment as per Class 10 results and seat availability.

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card of student and parent
  • Residence (domicile) certificate
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Transfer Certificate & previous mark sheet (Class 2 onward)
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs
