Swami Atmanand Government English Medium School

Sonhat, District - Koriya, Chhattisgarh

The Swami Atmanand Utkrisht Vidyalaya Yojana was launched by the Government of Chhattisgarh on Rajyotsava, 1st November 2020, to give children from every background — especially from rural and economically weaker families — access to high-quality English-medium education, completely free of cost.

Our school in Sonhat brings this vision to the children of Sonhat block and nearby villages. With smart classrooms, science and computer laboratories, a library, sports facilities and dedicated teachers, we prepare our students to compete confidently at the state and national level while staying rooted in our culture and values.

Dear students and parents, welcome to Swami Atmanand Government English Medium School, Sonhat. Our aim is to provide every child with quality education rooted in strong values, so that they grow into confident, responsible and compassionate citizens.

We believe each child is unique. Our teachers work to understand the needs of every student and help them discover their potential — in academics, sports, arts and life skills.
