Our School

Swami Atmanand Government English Medium School, Sonhat is part of the Swami Atmanand Utkrisht Vidyalaya Yojana of the Government of Chhattisgarh. The scheme was launched on 1st November 2020, Chhattisgarh Rajyotsava, so that children from every section of society — especially from rural, tribal and economically weaker families — can receive high-quality English-medium education free of cost.

Located in Sonhat block of Koriya district, the school serves students from Class 1 to Class 12 and offers modern facilities including smart classrooms, science and computer laboratories, a library and sports grounds.

🎯 Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that nurtures every child into a confident, capable and compassionate citizen, equipped with knowledge, skills and values for the 21st century.

🚀 Our Mission

  • Provide a creative, skill-based learning environment in English medium.
  • Develop scientific temperament, curiosity and critical thinking.
  • Build self-motivation, discipline and a sense of responsibility.
  • Instil respect for our culture, environment and community.
  • Ensure equal opportunity for every child, regardless of background.

About Swami Atmanand

Swami Atmanand (1929–1989) was a revered monk of the Ramakrishna Mission from Chhattisgarh, known for his work in education and the upliftment of tribal communities, particularly through the Ramakrishna Mission Ashram at Narayanpur. The Government of Chhattisgarh named this scheme of excellent schools in his memory to carry forward his ideals of service through education.
